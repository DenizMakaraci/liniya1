# Acme Scheduler overview

## What it does

Acme Scheduler is online appointment scheduling for small clinics and service businesses. Clients
book from a web page or link, and the business manages the calendar from a dashboard or phone.

## Reminders

Automatic appointment reminders go out by SMS and email. Reminder timing is configurable (for example
48 hours and 2 hours before). Clients can confirm, reschedule or cancel from the reminder.

## Calendar sync

Two-way sync with Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook. Appointments booked in Acme appear in the
staff member's calendar and busy times from the calendar block booking slots.

## Getting started

Setup takes about 20 minutes: connect a calendar, add services and durations, set working hours, and
share the booking link. The onboarding team offers a free 30-minute setup call during the trial.

## Data and privacy

Customer data is stored in Canada. Businesses can export or delete their data at any time from
Settings. Details are in the privacy policy at acme.example/privacy.

# Billing FAQ

## How do I change my billing details?

Log in to your Acme account, open Settings, then Billing, and choose "Update payment method". Changes
apply to the next invoice. Account owners and billing admins can make this change; other users will
see a read-only view. If you can't log in, a colleague can send a secure update link to the account
owner's email.

## When am I charged?

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance on the day you first subscribed. Invoices are emailed to
the billing contact and are available under Settings, then Billing, then Invoices.

## How do I cancel?

Cancel any time from Settings, then Billing, then "Cancel plan". Your account stays active until the
end of the current billing period. We don't charge cancellation fees.

## Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team case by case. The assistant cannot approve or promise
a refund; it can take the details and have the billing team follow up within two business days.
